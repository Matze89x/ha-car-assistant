# Screenshots

🇬🇧 English · [🇩🇪 Deutsch](screenshots.de.md)

This is what Car Assistant looks like in use (version 0.5.0 Beta). All pictures show a **demo car with made-up data** (a diesel and an electric car with two years of history); no real vehicle, no real places. You can create the demo car yourself: *Add vehicle → Demo car*.

## Dashboard

The car at a glance: odometer, range, messages of the car, next inspection, open points and widgets such as the last trip, fuel and consumption.

![Dashboard](screenshots/en/dashboard.png)

## Dashboard (electric)

The same page for an electric car: charging, battery and range instead of a tank.

![Dashboard (electric)](screenshots/en/dashboard-ev.png)

## Fuel

All fill-ups by month with consumption and prices; the station search sits on top.

![Fuel](screenshots/en/fuel.png)

## Evaluation (costs)

What the car costs in the chosen period: the total, the share of fuel, service, modifications and parts, a chart per period and a list of every priced entry.

![Evaluation](screenshots/en/report.png)

## Trips and trip analysis

The analysis on top (30 days, 90 days, year, all): trips by kind and length, a heat map of weekday and time of day, and the driving style; below it the km counters and the trips by month.

![Trips](screenshots/en/trips.png)

## Settings: User, Car, System

The settings are a pyramid of three tabs. **User** is about you (login, your devices, the cars you want to be notified about, a start link per car for a web app); **Car** is about the car shown under the title (icon, photo and video, design, Home Assistant entities, notifications, backup); **System** is about the whole app (language, units, design, sensors, MCP access, sources, address, backup).

![Settings: User](screenshots/en/settings-user.png)

![Settings: Car](screenshots/en/settings-car.png)

![Settings: System](screenshots/en/settings-system.png)
