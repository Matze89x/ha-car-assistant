**Drive · Log · Go**

Your car's own logbook and dashboard: vehicle logbook for fuel stops, service book, maintenance reminders, appointments, spare parts, documents, trips and your own km counters – for one or many cars, fully local. Every car has its own plugins - a plugin connects anything outside (a car, a cloud, a web service) through the Plugin API; modules can be switched on and off. The port is off until you set it; direct access always needs a user.

Open the Documentation tab for usage and configuration.
