**Drive · Log · Go**

Your car's own logbook and dashboard: fuel and charging, service book, maintenance reminders, appointments, spare parts, documents, trips and your own km counters – for one car or many, fully local. Use as much as you need: every car switches on only the functions it needs, from a plain fuel or charging log to everything. Every car has its own plugins - a plugin connects anything outside (a car, a cloud, a web service) through the Plugin API. The port is off until you set it; direct access always needs a user.

Open the Documentation tab for usage and configuration.
