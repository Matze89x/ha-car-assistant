# Code of conduct

🇬🇧 English · [🇩🇪 Deutsch](docs/community/code-of-conduct.de.md)

Car Assistant is a friendly project made in spare time. Everyone is welcome - whatever their experience, background or tools (a person, or a person working with an AI assistant).

- Be kind and patient. Disagree with the idea, never with the person.
- Assume good intent; ask when something is unclear.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks. No spam.
- Keep private information of others out of issues and pull requests - and your own.

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads and block people who do not follow these rules. Report a problem privately through the *Security* tab of the repository or on the contact given in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
